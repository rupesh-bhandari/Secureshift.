GAME DETAILS

SECURESHIFT is a workplace cybersecurity awareness simulation.

You are a junior IT/security employee starting your first shift.

Your job is to help employees identify suspicious emails, messages, websites and security events.

Click the sender / contact name to reveal full technical details — that's how you spot phishing.

HOW TO PLAY
  • WASD — Move
  • MOUSE — Look around
  • E — Interact with employees
  • ESC — Pause

Approach an employee and press E to inspect their workstation.

Click the sender name in Mail or Chat to reveal the real email address, reply-to address and SPF/DKIM status.

Click the EMPLOYEE ROSTER card (top-left) to expand the roster.
